# Heartbroken Helen (HH) — J1

*The grieving senior-dog owner (highest LTV).*

> "My dog hesitates at the stairs he used to race up, and watching him slow down is breaking my heart."

## One-line summary
Devoted, grieving owner watching her senior Labrador slow down; not price-sensitive, will do anything for more good time, converts on emotional resonance plus credible mechanism.

## Who they are
- **Age:** 54
- **Location:** Kloof, Durban
- **Job:** Office manager
- **Income:** R45,000/month household
- **Living situation:** Family home, garden with stairs
- **Kids:** Adult children, moved out
- **Relationship:** Married
- **Pet:** Labrador, 11 years old

## Core problem
Her dog has been with her through everything and watching him slow down is devastating. He hesitates at the stairs and calculates whether it's worth it. She has already spent heavily on X-rays and vet visits and will spend more for quality time. The emotional weight is enormous.

## Core pains & frustrations
- Watching a beloved senior dog visibly decline — the dog is family.
- Devoted, grieving, willing to do anything.
- Fear of false hope after spending so much already.

## Behavioural traits
Highest-value customer. When it works she tells everyone. Not price-sensitive. Converts on emotional resonance plus credible mechanism.

## Needs
Genuine help (not a cure), realistic hope, "more good time." Emotional validation.

## Barriers
Fear of false hope after spending so much already.

## Awareness level
Problem Aware / Solution Aware — actively seeking help for visible decline.

## Products that fit
- **Joint Support — PRIMARY.**

## Strategist notes
The "stood at the bottom of the stairs and just looked at them" image. Emotional hook, regenerative (not masking) mechanism, mobility guarantee.
