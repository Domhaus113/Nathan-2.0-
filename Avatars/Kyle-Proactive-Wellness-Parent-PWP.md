# Kyle — The Proactive Wellness Parent (PWP)

> "I want to give my pet the best — before problems even start."

## One-line summary
Health-conscious, science-respecting owner with no crisis; buys the vet-formulated, high-concentration version to prevent future problems and converts on mechanism, not pain.

## Who they are
Kyle doesn't have a crisis. His dog is healthy, for now. But he's seen what happens to friends' dogs who weren't supplemented properly. He follows Dr. Amir on TikTok and has watched the EPA/DHA content multiple times. He understands that commercial pet food doesn't have enough omega-3. He just wants the RIGHT product, not some generic supermarket brand — the vet-formulated, high-concentration version he can feel confident about. He'll spend R380 without blinking if the credibility signals are right.

## Core problem
Not a problem yet — an anxiety about the future. Kyle is afraid of being the person who could have prevented something and didn't. He's seen breed-specific health deterioration in dogs whose owners "didn't bother." He wants to be proactive but doesn't want to waste money on a product that's all marketing and no substance. He needs mechanism-level credibility before he'll buy.

## Core pains & frustrations
- **Intellectual pride** — "I take care of myself properly. Generic fish oil isn't good enough."
- **Anxiety** — "What if I'm missing something now that shows up in 3 years?"
- **Identity validation** — he's the type of person who gives their pet vet-formulated supplements.
- **Myth he holds** — thinks the omega-3 label on food covers it. Doesn't know about the ALA conversion problem (under 10%).
- **Needs mechanism proof** — he'll read the ingredient panel. The product page has to survive scrutiny.

- Education first, not pain. Respect his intelligence.
