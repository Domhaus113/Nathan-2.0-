# Terrified Tanya — The Post-Trauma Owner (TT)

> "I am never going through that dental procedure again."

## One-line summary
Just watched her senior dog go under general anaesthetic for a dental; buying prevention and control so she never repeats it — highest-LTV, lowest-friction dental segment.

## Who they are
Tanya has just watched her dog be put under general anaesthetic for a dental cleaning or extraction. For a senior dog that is genuinely frightening — the risk is real and she spent the whole morning staring at her phone waiting for the vet to call. She is not buying to prevent a problem; she is buying to make sure she NEVER experiences that again. The highest-LTV, lowest-friction segment in the dental market. Her motivation is protection and control.

## Core problem
The trauma of the GA event. The cost of the GA event. The fear of doing it again, especially as her dog gets older and the anaesthetic risk compounds. She is actively searching for a prevention mechanism. She will subscribe on first purchase.

## Core pains & frustrations
- **Post-GA trauma** — the waiting-for-the-call anxiety was unbearable.
- **Anaesthetic risk fear** — especially for senior dogs. Cannot do it again.
- **Financial shock** — R5,000–R9,000 out of pocket. No pet insurance in SA.
- **Wants control** — needs a daily prevention mechanism, not a reactive one.
- **Legacy anxiety** — "I want him to keep all his teeth." "I want to know I did everything I could."

## What she wants to hear
- "I wasn't going to let it get that far again."
- "No need for expensive dental care or anaesthesia."
- "The vet was so impressed with his teeth."
- "For the dog parent who knows every year counts."
- Frame as prevention and protection, not cosmetic.

## Awareness level
Problem Aware / Solution Aware — actively searching for a prevention mechanism.

## Products that fit
- **Dental Powder — PRIMARY.** Converts fastest of the four dental avatars.
- **Cross-sell Joint Support (stiffness) + Omega-3 (coat/cognition)** on the welcome flow — highest-LTV senior-dog stack customer.
