# Tarryn — The Desperate Allergy Parent (DAP)

> "I've tried everything. I just need something that actually works."

## One-line summary
Pharmaceutically-exhausted dog mum whose dog has chronic itching; burned before, but desperate enough to try one more thing if a vet and a guarantee back it.

## Who they are
Tarryn has been living with her dog's chronic itching for over a year. She's been down the full pharmaceutical road — cortisone, Apoquel, Cytopoint — and while some things worked temporarily, nothing lasted. She's financially strained by the cycle, emotionally exhausted, and carries a quiet, persistent guilt that she's failing her dog. She's sceptical of new products because she's been burned before, but she's also desperate enough to try one more thing, as long as there's a vet behind it and a guarantee backing it.

## Core problem
Her dog scratches constantly. Skin goes red and raw. It wakes them both up at night. She's cycling through expensive pharmaceutical treatments that only work for weeks at a time, worrying about long-term side effects, and feeling helpless — like there's no root-cause solution, just symptom management on an endless loop.

## Core pains & frustrations
- **Guilt** — "He's suffering and I'm his mum. I'm supposed to fix this. Every scratch feels like a personal failure."
- **Exhaustion** — 3am scratching keeping her awake. 18 months of this. Sleep-deprived and anxious.
- **Financial strain** — R1,200–R1,400 a month on Apoquel or Cytopoint. R15,000+ on treatments this year alone.
- **Resentment** — feels the vet system is keeping her on a subscription of injections with no real answers.
- **Scepticism** — burned before by products promising everything. Needs a vet name and a guarantee.
- **Desperation** — willing to try one more thing if the credibility signals are right.
