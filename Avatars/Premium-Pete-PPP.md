# Premium Pete — The Science-Driven Researcher (PPP)

> "Show me the mechanism or I'll buy from someone who will."

## One-line summary
Highly educated, ingredient-panel-reading researcher who benchmarks against PlaqueOff; sold on clinical ingredient storytelling, never emotion. Hardest to convert, highest referral value.

## Who they are
Pete is the science-driven researcher (both genders, often male-skewed). Highly educated. Reads ingredient panels. Compares products. He will find PlaqueOff — the global leader — read its VOHC (Veterinary Oral Health Council) status, and directly ask whether Vets2Pets is meaningfully different. This avatar is not sold with emotional guilt; he is sold with clinical ingredient storytelling: the 6-in-1 mechanism, each ingredient's role, Dr. Amir's formulation credibility.

## Core problem
Not a problem yet — a due-diligence question. He wants the optimal product, not the marketing product. If Vets2Pets doesn't survive his ingredient-panel comparison, he'll buy PlaqueOff. Medium conversion difficulty but highest organic share rate once he does buy.

## Core pains & frustrations
- **Scepticism of marketing** — doesn't want to be sold to.
- **Mechanism questions** — "how exactly does kelp reduce plaque?"
- **Competitor comparison** — PlaqueOff is the benchmark; he needs a real differentiator.
- **Value proof** — is R380 actually better than R280 PlaqueOff? Prove it.
- **Wants the founder story** — Dr. Amir's formulation credibility matters.

## What he wants to hear
- "Our 6-in-1 formula has 4 more active ingredients than PlaqueOff."
- "Dr. Amir formulated this because he couldn't find a local product with the right spec."
- Ingredient-by-ingredient breakdown. VOHC comparison addressed honestly. No emotional appeals.

## Awareness level
Product Aware / Most Aware — comparison-stage, due-diligence buyer.

## Products that fit
- **Dental Powder — PRIMARY.** 3-product stack candidate.

## Strategist notes
The organic-share avatar — hardest to convert, highest referral value. Lead with mechanism content (Dr. Amir educational TikTok / long-form carousel on the 6-in-1 formula). Do not lead with emotion. Once he buys, he subscribes and tells his network.
