# Breakeven Economics

## What it is
The real unit economics that decide whether a creative is winning or losing. All verdicts trace back to these numbers.

## The numbers (confirmed)
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| AOV (blended) | R750 |
| COGS | 60.95% (R457.13 / order) |
| Margin | 39.05% (R292.88 / order) |
| **Breakeven CPA** | **R292.88** |
| Breakeven ROAS | 2.56x |
| Target CPA | R150–R170 |
| Target ROAS | ~5.5x (theoretical; practical target is the CPA range) |

## Four-tier verdict system
| CPA Zone | Verdict | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Below R150 | Scaling | Green |
| R150–R170 | At target | Green |
| R171–R292 | Alive — not at target | Amber |
| Above R292 | Losing money | Red |

## How to apply it
- Judge every creative by its CPA against the four tiers first; ROAS is secondary context.
- "Breakeven" means R292.88 CPA / 2.56x ROAS — at this point the order contributes R0 margin.
- Anything above R292 CPA is losing money and should be cut or fixed unless there's a strong reason to keep testing.

- These supersede any placeholder economics in the Nathan app.
